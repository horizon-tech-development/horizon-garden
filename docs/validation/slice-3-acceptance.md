# Slice 3 acceptance: companion recommendations

## Required behavior

- Recommend nearby plants from the local catalog after a crop is selected.
- Treat relationships as directional rather than assuming both crops receive the same benefit.
- State the proposed benefit and practical rationale.
- Label every recommendation as supported, mixed, or traditional evidence.
- Show cautions when a familiar claim could be overstated.
- Never promise that a companion prevents insects or disease.
- Work entirely offline through shared deterministic domain rules.

External citations, regional rules, conflicts, user-authored relationships, and catalog provenance expansion remain later catalog work.
